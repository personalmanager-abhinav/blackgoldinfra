PatchFILL — The Pothole Repair Machine

Spray-injection pothole repair unit, Model C-PH-5M³ — fast, durable, compaction-free road repair.

Model C-PH-5M³

Fast, Durable, Intelligent Road Repair

PatchFILL uses advanced spray-injection technology with a rear-mounted hydraulic boom to repair potholes safely, with minimal traffic disruption and maximum productivity.

Its self-contained design carries everything needed for spray-injection patching on a single truck — compressor, flush, air and emulsion tanks, and a burner-heated emulsion system. The result is durable patches for highways, municipal roads, airports, industrial roads, parking areas, footpaths, cycle tracks and road shoulders.

The Process

Proven Spray-Injection Process

Strong, long-lasting repairs in five continuous steps — no conventional compaction required.

1

Clean

Clean the pothole thoroughly with high-pressure compressed air.

2

Tack Coat

Apply a uniform tack coat of bitumen emulsion.

3

Inject

Simultaneously inject coated aggregate and emulsion through the nozzle.

4

Placement

High-velocity placement forms a dense, well-bonded patch — no compaction.

5

Ready for Traffic

Surface is ready for traffic in a short time, cutting road closures.
